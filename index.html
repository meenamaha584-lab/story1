The Helpful Little Sparrow

On a sunny morning, a little sparrow was sitting on a tree near a beautiful stream. Suddenly, it noticed a tiny ant struggling in the flowing water. The ant was moving here and there but could not reach the river bank. It was slowly drifting away and was in great danger.

The sparrow felt sorry for the little ant and quickly decided to help it. It picked a fresh green leaf from the nearby tree and dropped it carefully into the water. The ant climbed onto the leaf and held it tightly. The leaf floated safely towards the river bank.

A few days later, a hunter came into the forest carrying a net. He saw the sparrow sitting on a branch and slowly aimed his net towards it. The sparrow did not notice the hunter and was unaware of the danger waiting nearby.

The little ant saw the hunter and remembered how the sparrow had saved its life. Without wasting time, the ant quickly ran towards the hunter and bit his foot. The hunter cried out loudly and dropped his net. Hearing the noise, the sparrow flew away safely.

Moral: Kindness is never wasted. A small help can make a big difference.
